About

What I do

I graduated in International Business from the University of Finance and Marketing (CGPA 3.4, three Academic Excellence Scholarships), and I work across the full marketing loop rather than one slice of it: producing the creative, choosing what gets budget, reading the performance data, and reporting the outcome.

Most recently at Vải Hoàng Kim I made the page's daily visuals and short-form videos in Canva and CapCut, ran boosted Facebook post campaigns on company budget, and reviewed the metrics every day to decide what to rebuild and what to drop.

Alongside that, I have run an English-language campaign end to end for a US home-fragrance brand, coordinated the administration and budget of a hybrid event for ~200 people, and completed two quantitative research studies in SmartPLS — including a 433-response dataset I collected, cleaned and analysed myself.
