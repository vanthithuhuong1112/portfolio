About

Creative first, then the numbers decide

I make the asset, put budget behind it, and check the next morning whether it earned its place. At Vải Hoàng Kim that meant producing the page's daily visuals and short-form videos in Canva and CapCut, choosing which posts to boost, and rebuilding the angles that worked instead of guessing the next one.

I also like the research half of the job. I ran a full campaign for Slow North, a home-fragrance brand in Austin, Texas — brochure, Facebook promotion, registration funnel, and an English-language session I presented myself — and screened an entire product category for a foreign market in the Vinamilk Mexico entry study.

Reading a table of numbers to decide what to scale and what to stop is the part I enjoy most, and it is backed by real practice: a 433-response dataset analysed end to end, and an event budget I tracked to 29% under plan.
