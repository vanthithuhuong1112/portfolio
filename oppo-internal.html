About

The programme, the artwork and the list behind it

Internal communication is three jobs at once — someone has to have the idea, someone has to make the poster, and someone has to know who registered, who showed up and what they thought afterwards. On the events I have run, that someone was me.

On the TopCV × UFM hybrid event I held administration and finance for around 200 participants: cost calculation, printed materials, standees, speaker and participant gifts, supplier coordination and on-the-day support — closed at 29% under the planned budget, with post-event follow-up actually closed out rather than left open. On the Slow North webinar I owned the whole cycle alone: agenda, brochure, registration form, Microsoft Teams setup, the live session, and the satisfaction survey afterwards.

The design half is daily work for me. At Vải Hoàng Kim I produced every graphic the page published, in Canva, for three months. And writing for an internal reader is what eighteen months as a teaching assistant actually was: keeping the running record and condensing it into short, clear updates for the person who needed to act on it — announcements and status notes under a different name.
